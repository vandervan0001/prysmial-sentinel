# IEC 62443 applicability and evidence

Identify the relevant part, edition and organizational role before mapping requirements: asset owner, integrator, product supplier or service provider. Public guides and summaries do not replace the applicable normative text.

1. Define the system, boundaries, zones and conduits against the process and permitted flows. A VLAN alone does not describe a complete security zone.
2. Relate risk analysis to security objectives and applicable requirements. Distinguish target level, component capabilities and the state actually achieved by the system.
3. Assign an owner, evidence, method and gap to each selected requirement. Separate process, integration and technical product requirements.
4. Check compensating controls and their operating dependencies: access management, maintenance, backups, monitoring and response. Documentation alone does not establish effectiveness.
5. If the normative text is unavailable, prepare a working matrix with references marked for confirmation. Do not invent requirement numbers or compliance conclusions.

Deliver a technical review and applicability matrix. Certification requires its own scope and process. Start with the [official ISA/IEC 62443 overview](https://www.isa.org/standards-and-publications/isa-standards/isa-iec-62443-series-of-standards), then consult the authorized normative edition for the engagement.
